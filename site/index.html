Draw a mechanism. Watch it move. Read the numbers.

Free teaching software for Mechanics of Machinery. LinkLab is for linkages and CamLab is for cams. Both run in your web browser.

Open LinkLab Open CamLab
  • No install
  • No login
  • Works offline after the first visit
  • Free and open source

Two apps

Each app follows the order the topic is taught, from the drawing to the numbers.

LinkLab Linkages

  • Draw with compass, protractor and straightedge, then build a linkage on top.
  • Position, velocity and acceleration of any point, plotted over the cycle.
  • Joint forces, input torque and shaking forces; balance with counterweights.
  • Export the links as DXF for SolidWorks or Inventor.

CamLab Cams

  • The graphical method step by step, next to the exact profile.
  • Motion laws from constant velocity to polynomials, with SVAJ plots.
  • Pressure angle, radius of curvature, spring force and follower jump.
  • Export a DXF spline, profile points, or an STL for 3-D printing.

For teachers

Made for a Mechanics of Machinery course and used in class.

One file per app

Download LinkLab.html or CamLab.html and share it with your class. It opens with a double-click and needs no internet.

Works on iPad

Open the app in Safari. To keep it, choose Share › Add to Home Screen.

Basic and Advanced

Start with motion only. Switch to Advanced when the class reaches forces, balancing and cam sizing.

Files stay with you

Designs are saved as files on your own device. The apps send nothing anywhere and need no account.

These are teaching aids. Check important results independently before using them to design a real machine.
